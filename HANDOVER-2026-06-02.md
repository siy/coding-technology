### Loth two-axis cohesion — the biggest content addition
New Loth paper: ***On the Nature of Cohesion*** (Zenodo, 2026-06-01, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20492913, CC BY 4.0). Cohesion = "correctness relative to a modularization principle," two independent axes: **purity** (no element the principle separates) and **completeness** (all elements it unites). Failure modes: purity-without-completeness = *shotgun surgery*; completeness-without-purity = *accidental coupling*. His LCOM critique (structure mistaken for cohesion) independently corroborates PFD's "shared-data view has the arrow backwards."

Threaded as **practical diagnostics** (not metrics theory):
- **Foundations** — the recognition test now has its two named directions + a credit paragraph (PFD's change driver *is* such a principle; structural metrics miss cohesion because they measure what code shares, not what governs it).
- **Spiral 2** — booking workflow shown passing both checks.
- **Spiral 3** — refund placement named a **purity** call.
- **Brownfield** — "boundaries that predate reasons" audit reframed as a purity-and-completeness audit.
- **References + Acknowledgments** — added as Loth's second work.

**Fidelity choice:** instantiated Loth's schema with PFD's change-driver as the principle; **deliberately omitted his SRP polemic** (Theme 25 positive-register guardrail) — credit the convergence, stay positive. Goal: Loth recognizes his framing doing real methodology work.

## Conventions in force (apply to all new drafting)

- **Vignette** (italic) at chapter top; **threads-advanced** tag at foot.
- **Em-dash ≤2 per prose paragraph** (definition lists and table cells exempt). Verified clean across all edits.
- **Forbidden vocab** in publishable body: "non-functional"/"NFR"/"system-level requirement"/"system-input requirement" — except the single deliberate naming-to-refuse at `architecture-synthesis.md` (the audit). Use "quality requirements"/"system qualities"; at detail, SLO/constraint/operational target/substrate-shaping.
- **Product names:** none, **except** Aether (where the slice deployment mode is load-bearing) and positive crediting (Jackson, King, Loth, Yevtushenko, Poltorak).
- **Register:** research-cited, sharp, positive — **no competitor/SOLID teardown** (this governed how Loth's SRP critique was *not* imported).
- **Code:** pseudo-code for type shapes, Java for composition; JBCT idioms strict (single-param `Result<T>`, `Promise<T>` carries failure, `Unit` not `Void`, `TypeName.typeName(...)` factories, method references, sealed `Cause`). Workflows are plain interfaces with an explicit `apply`, not a base type.
- **Cohesion vocabulary (new):** the change-driver recognition test has two directions — **completeness** (all the driver governs; miss → shotgun surgery) and **purity** (only what it governs; mix → accidental coupling). Both required.
