# Principles That Change Everything
## Less Art, More Engineering

Developers love principles. I'm not an exception. Below is my 5 principles that shape everything I do as a software engineer.

## One answer per question, or an explicit refusal
Determinism with its honesty clause

Our job as software engineers consists of decisions. A lot of them. The less unimportant decisions we need to make, the more time we have to important ones. But sometimes decision can't be made. Then we have two choices: make some decision anyway or explicitly say that decision can't be made. I chose explicit refusal. It makes problem visible and exposes gap. Gap in knowledge, requirements, SLO, etc.
