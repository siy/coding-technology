### Coverage, honestly

`coverage.test.js` pins it: **7 of 11 pressable ledger values have a press rule.** Unreached:
`unified runtime`, `serverless`, `streaming`, `sharded` — each needs a rule *and* a sheet
field (partition key, duty cycle, coupling/topology-uncertainty). The test fails if a ledger
value gains a `pressedBy` and nobody decides whether a rule reaches it.

**Known under-implementation:** `separated` requires three conditions together per
LEDGER.md:121 — own tight SLO AND own scale shape AND tolerable staleness. The rule reads
shape divergence alone. It reproduced two runs by luck of those sheets.

## Findings for the books (none applied)

**Architecture Synthesis:**
1. **Q2 is bundled** — a service-level error budget and per-operation criticality live at
   different scopes. Both worked examples answer at `system`; Card 1 says "per operation".
   Provisional ruling in `rules.js`, reversible. Fix belongs in Card 1.
2. **Divergence has no normal form** — three axes move on divergence *between* units, and the
   sheet has no way to express it, so `read_shape`, `diverges_on` and `diverges` had to be
   invented. Step 1 is called *normalize* and does not normalize: decomposition splits bundled
   *concepts* but not bundled *units*. One row per unit makes divergence computable.
3. **"Shape" collides** — Card 3's demand shape (volume/contention/burst/deadline) vs
   `axes-and-ledger.md:35`'s read shape. Same word, different axis, and the second has no
   question. By the membership criterion it should be elicited.
4. **Mechanism notes** — a demand contained by a named mechanism with no axis move
   (`three-profiles.md:33`). A third output type the engine had no place for.
5. **Four rule numberings disagree** — LEDGER.md (scope exclusion = 5, which is what the CH
   transcript cites), `axes-and-ledger.md:61` (= 3), `appendix-worksheet.md` (= 2), Card 5
   (unnumbered). The published transcript cites numbers that resolve against none of the book's.
6. **Card 2 should print the ledger** — the `:39` forward reference is unfulfilled, and the
   content exists in LEDGER.md.
7. `three-profiles.md:5` states the book's experimental condition in a subordinate clause in
   Part II. Denys read the whole book and asked what it was about.
