## blurb
Where data comes from when process leads: the whole record materializes on exactly three occasions, and each has an owner, a price, and a name.

## learn
- The three occasions a whole record materializes: the read path, a spanning invariant, erasure
- Invariant closure: what a spanning rule actually binds
- The five motions: minting, accretion, absorption, emancipation, severance
- Retention per field, because ownership is per field
- The honest limit: invariant-dense domains, and what the claim becomes there

## exercise
### Find the Closures | ~15 min
Take one entity from a system you maintain and list its fields with their writers. Group the fields
bound together by real cross-field rules - those groups are the invariant closures. Compare the
closures' sizes with the entity's: the difference is what entity-first modelling materialized without
a writer.
