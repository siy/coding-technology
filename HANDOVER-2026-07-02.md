## What PFD 2.0.0 is (the 1.7.0 → 2.0.0 arc)

- **1.7.0 — two-layer reading + condensed edition.** Every chapter carries an internal *spine* (its load-bearing claim sentences), marked in source with `**bold**`. Poltorak's epigraph added to *Closing / Honest scope*.
- **de-spine (build change).** Poltorak said inline bold hurts the deep read. Resolution: the spine is **internal markup only** — the full-book build strips it (renders clean prose), the condensed build extracts it. Source keeps `**`. So the full book has NO bold sentences (only short list labels remain); the skim lives entirely in the free condensed edition.
- **1.8.0 — Variation without branching** (*Edge Cases*): answers Poltorak's combinatorial-explosion objection — process-first dispatches too, as *bound policy* not inheritance; the use-case body stays a linear Sequencer because the variation is resolved at the boundary. Plus scope-band definition, tiers-overclaim fix, single-sitting softening.
- **1.9.0 — Where the structure comes from** (*Foundations*, after *Finding the change driver*): the centerpiece. Decomposition as a *structured conversation with the business* ("nothing about the business without the business"; the register is the transcript; structure precipitates from it like data from process); the descent bottoms out at Leaves; the inversion that the method *produces* the business model as a byproduct rather than requiring it as input ("nobody knows the whole business, and nobody has to"). Also removed the one-sitting-read claim entirely.
- **Loth / IVP reframe** (*Foundations / The telescope*): from defended priority ("corroboration, not foundation") to plain gratitude — the book has a working scheme from practice, Loth built the formal foundation, the credit for the rigor is his.
- **Vocabulary de-branded** (whole book): "the patterns / the shapes / the properties" replace "the six patterns / the four shapes / the six properties"; each count stated once where its members are listed; argument-carrying counts kept (recovery triple, two axes, one organizing structure). Readers were keeping the number, losing the content.
- **Afterword**: personal author's note — the density was the method leaking, unnoticed, into the prose. Deliberately kept OUT of the condensed edition (no spine markup); the one page where the prose relaxes.

## The two-layer build system (how it works)

- **Source of truth:** the manuscript, with spine sentences marked `**bold**` (indistinguishable in source from legitimate short bold labels, which is fine — the tools tell them apart by "is it a full sentence").
- `book-pfd-meta/extract-spine.py` — pulls bold *full-sentence* spans (skips short list-labels and list lines) → the condensed edition's content. ~315 spine sentences.
- `book-pfd-meta/despine.py` — strips `**` from spine sentences for the full-book build (leaves short labels bold). Run per-chapter into a temp dir by `build-pdf.sh`.
- `book-pfd-meta/build-pdf.sh --final` — builds the clean (de-spined) full book. Default is draft (DRAFT watermark). CHAPTERS list now includes `afterword.md` (after closing, before glossary).
- `book-pfd-meta/build-condensed.sh` — builds `process-first-design-condensed.pdf` ("The Argument in Twenty Minutes") from the extracted spine.
- **Version single-sourced** from the top `## [x.y.z]` entry of `book-pfd/CHANGELOG.md`.
