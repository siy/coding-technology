## Conventions in force (apply to all new drafting)

- **Vignette** (italic, observational + small turn) at chapter top; **threads-advanced tag** at chapter foot. (Voice doc §7 legitimizes foot position.)
- **Em-dash ≤2 per prose paragraph** (definition lists using "Term — definition" are exempt).
- **Forbidden:** "non-functional"/"NFR"; "domain resource" (use change-driver cohesion); transit vocab (fares/transit/route — ticketing uses prices/tier prices/sales); DDD/competitor/SOLID teardown register (present positively).
- **Code:** pseudo-code for type shapes, Java for composition; JBCT idioms (single-param `Result<T>`, `Promise<T>` carries failure, I/O async, `Unit` not `Void`, `TypeName.typeName(...)` factories, method references, no `Promise<Result<T>>`, sealed `Cause`). Architecture Synthesis is largely code-free (vectors/judgment, not implementation).
- **Composed-not-invented:** a unit owns only what its composition adds; it inherits its constituents' types.
- **Grouping beat** (Passes 2-4 only — formed-from-below altitudes): show what's grouped + change-driver character + recognition test. The use case is the floor (no grouping beat). Within-altitude composition (six patterns, every altitude) ≠ cross-altitude grouping (change-driver cohesion, transitions only).
- **Register:** research-cited, sharp, observational; show-don't-tell; uniform commitment, varied form.
