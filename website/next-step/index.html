next_step — the entry gate

The book claims the derivation is mechanical and the judgment points are named. This page is the first half of that claim as an executable: it runs the entry gate over an answer sheet and reports, in the book's own vocabulary, what is not yet an answer.

What this does. Checks the five disciplines of the entry gate — priced, scoped, decomposed, triaged, surfaced — and cites the card behind each refusal.

What it will not do. It derives no vector, forces no axis, and resolves no judgment point. Targets, recovery ties, contradiction choices and product picks are the book's named refusals; a tool that answered them would overclaim exactly the way the book warns against. An UNKNOWN is a valid answer here, recorded and never guessed.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
