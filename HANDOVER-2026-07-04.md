- **Rendering-neutrality proven** by despine-diff: full book text identical to published 2.0.0 except that one sentence (bold/italic placement differs; 154pp pagination unchanged).
- Condensed grew 19pp/315 sentences → **24pp/367 (~7.7k words, ~25–30 min)**. User decided: keep as is; "Twenty Minutes" stays as figure of speech.
- Republished BOTH editions as 2.0.1 (PR #25; CHANGELOG entry).

**Spine-editing mechanics (for future passes):**
- `extract-spine.py` and `despine.py` share one predicate: bold span = spine iff terminal `.?!` OR ≥5 words; on list lines ≥5 words only. They MUST stay in lockstep.
- Bold-toggle edits (extend/add/remove `**`) are rendering-neutral for the full book — spine spans are stripped there anyway. Extending a short list-label bold to cover its definition costs the full book that label's bold weight (accepted trade).
- **Never end a bold span in `*italic*` adjacency** (`...?***`) — the lazy `\*\*(.+?)\*\*` regex leaves a stray `*` after despine. Strip inner italics inside such spans.
- Build: `book-pfd-meta/build-condensed.sh` / `build-pdf.sh --final`; version from top of `book-pfd/CHANGELOG.md`.

**Leanpub verification gotchas** (also in memory `project_release_workflow.md`): `pdf_preview_url` in the book JSON is the author-preview, NOT the sample — may 404 forever. Verify the sample via the public book page's `samples.leanpub.com/<slug>-sample.pdf` link + `cmp` against local. `page_count_published` reflects the full edition only. Rate limit 3 ops/min; publish returns benign "Unexpected Server Error"; re-POST `publish.json` via curl if rate-limited mid-script.
