# Afterword

To anyone who made it this far: I know. The book is dense. You felt every sentence, because each one was built to carry a load — there is nothing here to skim, no soft stretch to coast through, and over fifty thousand words that is genuinely tiring. I owe you the honest reason it turned out this way.

I did not plan it. Somewhere in the writing the method got into the prose without my noticing. The rule the book presses on your code — let nothing in that has not earned its place, keep every part load-bearing, add no structure you are only guessing you will need — I had, it turns out, been quietly applying to the sentences. I was writing the book the way the book tells you to build, and I never decided to. Form became content behind my back.

By the time I noticed, it was too late. The whole thing was already made this way, and to loosen it I would have had to unpick the one property that makes it what it is. So I left it. The density is a confession and a demonstration in the same breath: the book is the thing it describes.

If you would have taken it lighter, that version does exist — the argument alone, in about twenty minutes. But you came the long way, which means you read every load-bearing line of it. Thank you for the time. It is the best thing a book like this could ask for.

— S.Y.
